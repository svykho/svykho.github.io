Hi, I'm Alejandro — a physicist trying to understand how compact objects like black holes behave when they are perturbed.

I'm a PhD student in the Gravity Group at the IAC3 (Institute of Applied Computing & Community Code) at the University of the Balearic Islands.

I work on numerical simulations in General Relativity, exploring quasi-normal modes and gravitational waves.
My interests are:
Black Holes · Numerical Relativity · Quasi-Normal Modes · Gravitational Waves · Nonlinear Effects
