About

Hello

Hi, I'm Alejandro, a physicist trying to understand how compact objects like black holes behave when they are perturbed. I'm a PhD student in the Strong Gravity Group at the IAC3 (Institute of Applied Computing & Community Code) of the University of the Balearic Islands. I work on numerical simulations in General Relativity, exploring quasinormal modes and gravitational waves.

Interests

  • Black holes
  • Numerical relativity
  • Quasinormal modes
  • Gravitational waves
  • Nonlinear effects
