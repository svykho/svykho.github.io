Black Hole Lab

Three simulators that run in your browser, and a short course that explains what they show.

Simulators

  1. 01

    Regge–Wheeler

    Send a Gaussian pulse at a Schwarzschild black hole and watch it scatter. The equation is solved live, and you can compare the ringing with the quasinormal-mode prediction.

    Open simulator →
  2. 02

    Sasaki–Nakamura

    Drop a small body into a Kerr black hole, down the axis or spiralling in from the innermost stable orbit, and see the waves and the ringdown it leaves behind.

    Open simulator →
  3. 03

    Wormhole

    Send a wave packet through a thin-shell wormhole between two universes. Part bounces back, part is trapped and leaks out as echoes, and part comes out on the other side.

    Open simulator →
